12 Rechargeable Fan Controller

AC + battery dual-power fan platform with automatic backup and dual-motor PWM control.

Category: Appliance Electronics, Power Electronics Role: Assistant Director (R&I Hardware), Walton Hi-Tech Industries PLC, Bangladesh

Project Description

Developed a microcontroller-based rechargeable fan control system designed for uninterrupted operation in residential and commercial environments with unreliable power supply conditions. The system integrates AC mains operation, battery backup, motor control, and intelligent power management into a single low-cost embedded platform.

The design is based on the STM32G0 series microcontroller and enables seamless transition between AC power and 12 V lead-acid battery operation. During normal conditions, the system operates the fan while simultaneously charging the battery through an SMPS-based power stage. In case of AC failure, the system automatically switches to battery mode to ensure continuous operation without user intervention.

A dual-motor architecture is implemented to independently control the main fan motor and the oscillation (swing) motor. PWM-based control at 12 kHz is used to regulate fan speed efficiently using a totem-pole driver stage, ensuring smooth operation and improved energy efficiency.

The user interface is designed with a simple and robust three-button control system, allowing intuitive operation for power control, speed adjustment, and swing mode activation.

Technical Highlights

  • STM32G0-series low-cost microcontroller-based control system
  • Dual power-source architecture — AC mains + 12 V lead-acid battery backup
  • Automatic power switching between AC and battery operation
  • SMPS-based power supply for system operation and battery charging
  • Integrated lead-acid Battery Management System (BMS) for protection and charging control
  • Simultaneous fan operation and battery charging during AC mode
  • PWM-based fan-speed control at 12 kHz switching frequency
  • Totem-pole driver stage for efficient fan-motor driving
  • Dual motor control system:
  • Main fan motor speed control
  • Oscillation (swing) motor control
  • Three-button user interface:
  • Power ON / OFF control
  • Fan speed selection
  • Oscillation (swing) control
  • Low-voltage battery protection and cutoff mechanism
  • Power-failure detection with automatic backup activation
  • Optimised embedded firmware for real-time control and power efficiency
  • Low-cost appliance-grade PCB design for mass production

Project Outcome

  • Delivered a reliable rechargeable fan system capable of uninterrupted operation during power outages
  • Improved overall energy efficiency through optimised PWM motor control
  • Achieved seamless AC-to-battery transition without user impact
  • Extended battery lifespan through controlled charging and protection logic
  • Reduced system cost while maintaining industrial-grade reliability
  • Enabled scalable design for multiple rechargeable fan product variants
