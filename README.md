## About

Embedded hardware and PCB design engineer with **over 5 years of experience** delivering reliable, high-performance electronic products across industrial, IoT, automotive, and consumer markets.

I specialise in **multi-layer and HDI PCB design**, **microprocessor-based System-on-Module (SOM) development**, **high-speed and mixed-signal PCB design**, **power electronics**, and **signal/power integrity**. I take products end-to-end — from concept and schematic capture, through layout and simulation, to **high-speed and mixed-signal testing in the lab**, **prototype bring-up**, and **production support** — while leading design teams and collaborating cross-functionally with QA, QC, and manufacturing.

> **Currently open to new opportunities in hardware design, embedded systems, and PCB engineering.**

- Simultaneous fan operation and battery charging during AC mode
- PWM-based fan-speed control at 12 kHz switching frequency
- Totem-pole driver stage for efficient fan-motor driving
- Dual motor control system:
 - Main fan motor speed control

- Three-button user interface:
